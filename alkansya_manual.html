Panel 2 • Architecture

⚙️ How the System Works

The project brings together hardware and software into three main parts:

  • The Vault: An ESP32 microcontroller with push buttons, an OLED screen, and an infrared sensor.
  • Wi-Fi Connection: The ESP32 sets up its own wireless network (Alkansya-CoinCounter) so it can talk directly to your computer.
  • Web Dashboard: A local XAMPP server runs PHP and MySQL to store your deposits and display your live totals.
Panel 3 • Operation

🚀 Making a Deposit

Follow these steps whenever you drop a coin into the box:

  • Step 1: Press the button for your coin value (₱1, ₱5, ₱10, or ₱20).
  • Step 2: Look at the OLED screen for the INSERT PX prompt. You have 10 seconds.
  • Step 3: Drop the coin through the slot. The infrared sensor catches it as it falls.
  • Step 4: The system records the deposit and your laptop dashboard updates right away.
Panel 4 • Hardware

⚡ Wiring Reference

All electronic parts come from the LAFVIN ESP32 Starter Kit:

OLED Display: SDA (D21) | SCL (D22)
IR Obstacle Sensor: OUT (D18)
Coin Buttons: D13 (₱1), D12 (₱5), D14 (₱10), D27 (₱20)
Power: 3.3V and GND rails.

The buttons use internal pull-ups (INPUT_PULLUP), so no extra resistors are required.

Panel 5 • Web App

📊 The Web Dashboard

Open your browser to manage and review your savings:

  • Bento Grid: See your total savings, coin counts per denomination, and percentage breakdown bars.
  • Wrong Coin Correction: Click "Wrong coin inserted?" on the dashboard to fix your last deposit if you pressed the wrong button.
  • Goals & History: Manage savings targets, check past logs with pagination, and handle withdrawals.
Panel 6 • Troubleshooting

🛠️ Troubleshooting & FAQ

Quick solutions if something isn't working:

  • Device offline? Check that your computer is connected to the Alkansya-CoinCounter Wi-Fi network.
  • Server error? Make sure XAMPP Apache and MySQL are running and your server URL points to 192.168.4.2.
  • Timeout? If 10 seconds pass without dropping a coin after pressing a button, the device resets back to idle.
